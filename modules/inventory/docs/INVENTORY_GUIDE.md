# Inventory Management Module Guide (v3.1)

## Overview
The Inventory Management Module is an enterprise-grade, pluggable business engine designed for standalone operation or deep integration into modern SaaS and ERP systems.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Catalog & Hierarchy**: Categories, OEM Brands, Units of Measure (UOM) with conversion matrices, and SKU variations.
2. **Physical Topologies**: Multi-facility, zone, aisle, rack, and bin addressability.
3. **Double-Entry Ledger**: Real-time stock balance locks and strict append-only audit trail.
4. **Moving Weighted Average Valuation (WAV)**: Real-time unit cost recalculation on inbound receipts.
5. **Batch & Expiry Watch**: FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out) tracking, certificate attachment, and quality quarantine holds.
6. **Piece-Level Serialization**: Individual asset tracking, warranty timelines, and chain-of-custody history.
7. **Physical Stock Reconciliation**: Snapshot freeze, cycle count discrepancy identification, and dual-entry ledger adjustment posting.
8. **Automated Replenishment**: Min/Max thresholds, reorder triggers, and draft procurement suggestions.
